Privacy Policy

Aara Tamil Calendar (ஆரா காலண்டர்) · Android app · package com.aara.tamilcalendar

Effective date: 11 November 2026

In short

  • The app has no sign-up or login, and we do not collect your name, phone number, email, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • Your settings (city, your birth star and raasi, reminder choices) stay on your phone.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers and usage data to show and measure ads — see "Advertising" below.

Information stored on your device

  • Settings: the city you pick from the app's list, your birth star (நட்சத்திரம்) and raasi if you add them, and which reminders you turned on. These are used only to calculate timings and show reminders on your phone. They are never sent to us.
  • Cached data: the latest daily rates file and downloaded temple photos are kept in the app's storage so the app works offline.
  • Backup: if Android backup is turned on in your phone settings, Android may include these settings in your own Google account backup. This is controlled by your phone, not by us.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of this from your phone.

Internet use

The panchangam, calendar, festivals and porutham work offline. The app connects to the internet only to:

  • Download today's gold, silver, fuel and US dollar rates — a small public file from GitHub Pages (github.io). No personal information is sent; like any web request, GitHub's servers see your IP address. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Show temple photos — downloaded from Wikimedia Commons (wikimedia.org). See the Wikimedia privacy policy.
  • Show ads — through Google AdMob (below).

Tapping a temple's map or Wikipedia link opens Google Maps or your browser, which follow their own privacy policies.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and supported by ads from Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID,
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • information about your device and app usage, such as ad views and clicks, and crash or performance data related to ads.

This data is collected and used by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off personalised ads, in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads). In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change it any time from the app's Settings screen ("விளம்பர தனியுரிமை அமைப்புகள்").

Notifications

Daily panchangam, festival and Rahu Kalam reminders are created on your phone, only if you turn them on. They need Android's notification permission, which you can withdraw any time in your phone settings.

Children

The app is a general-audience calendar and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not receive or store any personal information on our own servers. The only third party that collects data through the app is Google, for advertising, as described above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
